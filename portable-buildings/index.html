Portable Buildings

Wood portable buildings arrive complete. They are built in a shop, trucked to your property, and set on your prepared spot in a single visit — no assembly, no kit, no crew. Because they sit on treated skids rather than a poured foundation, most sites need nothing more than level ground, and the building can be relocated later if you move.

How they are built

The same construction under every model

Styling and layout change between models. What holds them up does not.

Floor

Built not to sag or rot

  • Treated skids and joists on 16" centers, rated for ground contact
  • 4x6 notched floor skids — two on 8' wide buildings, four on 10' and 12' wide
  • 5/8" LP ProStruct treated floor decking with interlocking SmartFinish
  • Pulling blocks at the centermost skids so the building can be moved without damage

Walls

Finished, painted, warranted

  • LP SmartSide engineered wood siding and trim, treated against water and insects
  • 2x4 studs on 16" centers
  • Metal hurricane straps tying wall framing to rafters, rated to 160 mph
  • Glidden exterior paint in a choice of eleven colors
  • Ring-shank coated nails throughout

Roof

Shed water, vent heat

  • 2x4 rafters joined with steel plates
  • 7/16" OSB roof decking
  • Aluminum drip edge protecting decking and eaves
  • Ridge vent to let heat escape at the peak
  • Choice of dimensional shingles or a metal roof

Doors

Hardware that survives daily use

  • 6" heavy-duty zinc-plated hinges and handle
  • Adjustable tension rods to stop the doors sagging
  • Aluminum threshold protecting the floor edge
  • Storm chains to prevent wind damage
  • Takes a standard padlock without modification

Finishes

11 paint colours, 4 shingles, 23 metal roofs

Every building is painted and roofed at the shop, so it arrives finished — there is nothing to prime, coat or seal after it lands. Pick the combination when you order: it costs the same whichever you choose, and no colour carries a surcharge or a longer lead time.

Warranty

What is covered

  • Standard buildings — 5-year limited warranty on workmanship and materials.
  • Specialty buildings — 1-year limited warranty on Greenhouse, K-9 Condo, Chicken Coop and Playhouse models.
  • Siding — LP SmartSide siding and trim carries a 50-year manufacturer warranty.
  • Shingles — Dimensional shingles carry a manufacturer lifetime warranty.

Warranty runs to the original purchaser and requires the original serial tag to remain attached and visible. It does not cover installation or reinstallation costs, or the natural tendency of wood to split, warp or twist.
