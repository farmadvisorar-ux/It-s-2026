Why steel

Built to a spec, not to a guess

Factory-direct pricing

Kits ship from the plant nearest your jobsite. No middle distributor markup between the mill and your slab.

Engineer-stamped drawings

Every building is engineered to the wind, snow and seismic values at your specific address, and stamped for your jurisdiction.

Bolt-together assembly

Pre-punched panels and numbered parts. No welding, no specialized tooling — many owners raise their own building.

Non-combustible steel

100% steel construction. Nothing to rot, nothing termites will eat, and nothing that feeds a fire.
