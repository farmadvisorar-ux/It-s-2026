About the B Model

Vertical sidewalls with a low-slope roof. Usable floor area runs right to the wall line, which makes this the practical choice when you are lining walls with benches, racking or parked equipment.

Full-height walls mean standard overhead doors can be placed anywhere along the sidewall, not just the endwall.
