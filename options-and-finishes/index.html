Options & Finishes

Almost everything here is cheaper decided now than added later. Openings are framed into the engineering, insulation affects the envelope, and anchors are sized with the foundation — none of which is easy to revisit once the steel is cut.

Arch Building Options

Options and accessories for self-supporting arch and quonset structures. Because the arch is both frame and skin, every opening cut into it has to be framed and reinforced — which is why openings are specified at order time rather than added later.

Foundations

Foundations

Concrete slab, continuous footing, pier, or engineered alternatives sized to your soil and frost depth.

Insulation

Blanket, batt and spray foam systems for arch profiles, plus vapor control to manage condensation.

Man Doors

Walk-through steel entry doors, pre-framed into the endwall or arch.

Overhead Doors

Sectional roll-up doors sized for vehicles and equipment, with framed and reinforced openings.

Sliding Doors

Track-mounted sliding doors for wide agricultural and equipment openings.

Skylights

Translucent panels set into the arch for daylight without electrical load.

Turbine Vents

Wind-driven roof ventilators that exhaust hot, moist air without power.

Straight Wall Building Options

Finishes and accessories for straight-wall structures. Vertical walls accept standard doors, windows and trim in conventional positions, which makes straight-wall buildings considerably easier to customize than arches.

Color Options

Factory-finish color for roof, walls and trim, coordinated as a single scheme.
