Storage

Storage Buildings

Dry, secure, and not made of vinyl

Quote this building See ready-to-ship kits

Steel storage buildings solve the problem fabric and plastic structures do not: they stay put, they stay dry, and they can be locked. For anything valuable enough to insure, the difference matters.

What to think about before you order

The specification that pays for itself is ventilation. Sealed metal buildings holding equipment through temperature swings will condense moisture on the underside of the roof unless air can move. Vents or a vapor barrier are not optional in most climates.

Common uses

  • Seasonal and household storage
  • Equipment and tool storage
  • Inventory overflow
  • Secure site storage
