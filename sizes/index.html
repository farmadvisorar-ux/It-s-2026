Width is the expensive dimension

Adding length is comparatively cheap — you are repeating a bay that is already engineered. Adding width means a longer span and more steel throughout. If you are weighing 30' x 60' against 40' x 45', the first is usually cheaper for similar area.

Height is the one people regret

Sidewall height is the cheapest capacity you will ever buy, and the most expensive to add afterward — which is to say, impossible. Two extra feet can mean another rack level across the whole floor, or clearance for a lift.

Plan for the door, not the vehicle

Roof racks, raised loader arms and boats on trailers all need more clearance than the vehicle alone suggests. Size the opening to the largest thing that will ever pass through it, then add margin.
